🐴 Hero Horse

The horse runs on the ground. Jump (⬆️ / Up) over the fat enemies. Land on top of one to squash it flat, but touch it any other way and you lose a life!
Shoot arrows with Space and throw bombs with Alt. Arrows, bombs and armor float along the path — grab them! Armor also gives you a lance to smash enemies with.
